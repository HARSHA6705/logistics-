Custom Broker Services

Expert Customs Documentation & Cargo Clearance Under Licensed Operations

At Maxxwell Logistics, our Custom Broker services ensure seamless handling of both export and import shipments by Sea and Air. With decades of expertise and direct connectivity to Customs EDI systems in major hubs like Mumbai and Delhi, we offer clients a hassle-free, compliant, and efficient cargo clearance experience.

Our dedicated team of Customs House Agents (CHA) and specialists provides end-to-end support for documentation, clearance, and logistics, backed by advanced technology and an extensive network of associates worldwide.

Our custom broker services include

  • Clearing & forwarding of export/import consignments (Air & Sea)
  • Customs EDI registration & compliance
  • Container consolidation, dock de-stuffing/devanning
  • LCL (Less-than-Container Load) & FCL (Full Container Load) freight booking
  • House/factory stuffing consignments nationwide
  • House-to-house freight forwarding & door delivery
  • Private warehousing for export/import shipments
  • Duty calculations, bonds, LUT & bank guarantee execution
  • Drawback claims, Octroi-exemption (N-Form)
  • Logging export entries in DEEC books / passbooks
  • Proof of exports & DEPB license verification
  • Fumigation & phytosanitary certification of export consignments
  • Assistance with SGS inspections
  • Certificate of Origin / GSP Form A from Chamber of Commerce
  • Document legalization from consulates
  • Transportation & pick-up services

Why choose Maxxwell as your custom broker?

  • Licensed operations
  • Over a decade of trusted CHA expertise
  • Direct EDI connectivity with Indian Customs (Mumbai & Delhi)
  • Tailored solutions for complex compliance needs
  • Advanced in-house software & networked EDP systems for faster communication

We pride ourselves on being one of the pioneering CHAs in India, offering precision, speed, and compliance in every step of the clearance process.
